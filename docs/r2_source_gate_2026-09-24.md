## Adapter verification against source bytes

Its `winlog.event_data` mapping also misses top-level process fields in this archive. A source-specific, tested mapping is required before claiming endpoint rows. The archive hash belongs to the ZIP path; the exact member name belongs to `archive_member` in the manifest.
